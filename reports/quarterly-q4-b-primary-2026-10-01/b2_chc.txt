Energy Storage Platform Backed by Stonepeak and CHC Awarded Five Projects in Japan Long-term Decarbonization Auction
The energy storage platform jointly established by Stonepeak and CHC (the “Platform”) today announced that it has secured 20-year fixed revenue capacity market contracts for five battery energy storage system (“BESS”) projects totaling 348MW of gross capacity in the latest round of Japan’s Long-term Decarbonization Auction (the “Auction”).
